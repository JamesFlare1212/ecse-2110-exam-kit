## Question

4.30 A permanent magnet dc motor drives a mechanical load requiring a constant torque of 25 N · m. The motor produces 10 N · m with an armature current of 10 A. The resistance of the armature circuit is $0.2\,\Omega$. A 200 V dc supply is applied to the armature terminals. Determine the speed of the motor.

## Guide

Use the measured torque/current pair to find the constant magnetic coupling. The required load torque fixes the armature current; subtract its resistive voltage drop before converting back emf to speed.

## Part a

Determine the speed of the motor.

### Solution

Use the reference sheet's [DC torque relation](formula:dc-torque) and [generated-voltage relation](formula:dc-emf), with Kirchhoff's voltage law for the motor armature:

$$
T=K_a\Phi I_a,\qquad E_a=K_a\Phi\omega_m,\qquad V=E_a+I_aR_a
$$

The permanent magnet fixes $K_a\Phi$. The given calibration is $10\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ at $10\,\mathrm A$; at steady speed the developed torque equals the $25\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ load torque, neglecting unspecified rotational losses:

$$
\begin{aligned}
K_a\Phi&=\frac{10}{10}=1\,\mathrm{N\,m/A}=1\,\mathrm{V\,s/rad},\\
I_a&=\frac{25}{1}=25\,\mathrm A,\\
E_a&=200-25(0.2)=195\,\mathrm V,\\
\omega_m&=\frac{195}{1}=195\,\mathrm{rad/s}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Convert radians per second to revolutions per minute:

$$
n=\frac{60\omega_m}{2\pi}=\frac{60(195)}{2\pi}=\boxed{1862.11\,\mathrm{rpm}}.
$$
