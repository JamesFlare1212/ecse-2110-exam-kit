## Question

A three-phase, star connected load with a resistance of $30\,\Omega$ and a reactance of $25\,\Omega$ per phase, is fed by a $230\,\mathrm V$, three-phase, $60\,\mathrm{Hz}$ source. Calculate

## Guide

Treat $230\,\mathrm V$ as the line-to-line RMS voltage, consistent with the source solution. First solve the star load using its phase voltage. The delta capacitor branches each receive $230\,\mathrm V$, and their negative reactive power adds to the star load's positive reactive power.

## Part a

all line currents

### Solution

Use the [star line/phase relations](formula:star), $V_\phi=V_L/\sqrt3$ and $I_L=I_\phi$, together with phasor Ohm's law:

$$
I_a=\frac{V_{an}}{Z_Y},\qquad Z_Y=30+j25\,\Omega
$$

Choose positive sequence and $V_{an}=V_\phi\angle0^\circ$. The given voltage and impedance yield

$$
\begin{aligned}
V_\phi&=\frac{230}{\sqrt3}=132.7906\,\mathrm V\\
|Z_Y|&=\sqrt{30^2+25^2}=39.0512\,\Omega,\qquad \theta=\tan^{-1}\frac{25}{30}=39.8056^\circ\\
I_a&=\frac{132.7906}{30+j25}=2.61227-j2.17690\,\mathrm A
\end{aligned}
$$

Apply the same phase shifts as the source voltages:

$$
\boxed{I_a=3.40042\angle-39.8056^\circ\,\mathrm A,\quad
I_b=3.40042\angle-159.8056^\circ\,\mathrm A,\quad
I_c=3.40042\angle80.1944^\circ\,\mathrm A}
$$

## Part b

power consumed and

### Solution

Use the [three-phase real-power relation](formula:three-phase-power). For identical impedance branches it is equivalently

$$
P=3V_\phi I_\phi\cos\theta=3I_\phi^2R
$$

Substitute the unrounded star current from part (a) and the given $R=30\,\Omega$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
P&=3\left(\frac{230}{\sqrt3\sqrt{30^2+25^2}}\right)^2(30)\\
&=\frac{230^2(30)}{1525}=\boxed{1040.66\,\mathrm W}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Part c

power factor.

### Solution

For $Z=R+jX$, the power-factor angle is the impedance angle, so

$$
\mathrm{pf}=\cos\theta=\frac{R}{\sqrt{R^2+X^2}}
$$

The reactance is positive, so current lags voltage. Substitution gives

$$
\mathrm{pf}=\frac{30}{\sqrt{30^2+25^2}}=\boxed{0.768221\quad\text{lagging}}
$$

## Part d

If a delta connected, balanced $3$-phase capacitor bank, is added to the same supply in parallel to the given load, with the $15\,\text{micro Farad}$ capacitance/phase, then calculate the new overall power factor of the circuit.

### Solution

Use the capacitor law $I_C=j\omega CV$ and [Lecture 2, page 13](/pages/lecture-02/13.webp)'s $S=VI^*$. Because each delta capacitor sees $V_L$, its three-phase reactive power is

$$
Q_C=-3\omega C V_L^2,\quad Q_Y=3I_\phi^2X,\quad
\mathrm{pf}_{\mathrm{new}}=\frac{P}{\sqrt{P^2+(Q_Y+Q_C)^2}}
$$

Given $f=60\,\mathrm{Hz}$, $C=15\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm F$ per delta branch and $V_L=230\,\mathrm V$, use the unchanged real power from part (b):

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_Y&=\frac{230^2(25)}{30^2+25^2}=867.213\,\mathrm{var}\\
Q_C&=-3(2\pi\cdot60)(15\times10^{-6})(230)^2=-897.427\,\mathrm{var}\\
Q_{\mathrm{net}}&=867.213-897.427=-30.2142\,\mathrm{var}
\end{aligned}
$$

The small negative net reactive power means slight overcompensation:

$$
\mathrm{pf}_{\mathrm{new}}=\frac{1040.6557}{\sqrt{1040.6557^2+(-30.2142)^2}}
=\boxed{0.999579\quad\text{leading}}
$$

### Source correction

PDF page 9 gives $3.36\,\mathrm A$, $1028.34\,\mathrm W$ and $856.78\,\mathrm{var}$ for the original load. Direct calculation from its printed $30+j25\,\Omega$ and $230\,\mathrm V$ gives $3.40042\,\mathrm A$, $1040.66\,\mathrm W$ and $867.213\,\mathrm{var}$. The capacitor bank does not give exactly unity power factor: it slightly overcompensates. The source also writes a cosine where a sine is required in a reactive-power substitution. The final capacitor question has no printed letter; editorial (d) preserves it as a separate complete request, after the original (a)–(c).
