## Question

2.4. An inductive load consisting of $R$ and $X$ in parallel feeding from a 2400-V rms supply absorbs 288 kW at a lagging power factor of 0.8. Determine $R$ and $X$.

## Guide

The original diagram has a resistor and inductor on separate parallel branches. Each branch sees 2400 V. The resistor absorbs the real power and the ideal inductor absorbs the reactive power, so use voltage-squared relations rather than the series current from the preceding problem.

## Part a

Determine $R$ and $X$.

### Solution

Use Lecture 2, page 13's $S=VI^*$ and branch Ohm's law. A resistor current is $V/R$ and an inductive current is $V/(jX)$, hence

$$
S=V\left(\frac{V}{R}+\frac{V}{jX}\right)^*=\frac{V^2}{R}+j\frac{V^2}{X}
$$

The given $P=288000\,\mathrm W$, lagging power factor 0.8 and $V=2400\,\mathrm V$ imply

$$
\begin{aligned}
\theta&=\cos^{-1}0.8=36.8699^\circ\\
Q&=P\tan\theta=288000(0.75)=216000\,\mathrm{var}\\
R&=\frac{V^2}{P},\qquad X=\frac{V^2}{Q}
\end{aligned}
$$

Substitute the common branch voltage:

$$
\boxed{R=\frac{2400^2}{288000}=20\,\Omega,\qquad X=\frac{2400^2}{216000}=26.6667\,\Omega}
$$
