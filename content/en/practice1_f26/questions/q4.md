## Question

Two loads connected in parallel are supplied from a single-phase $240\text{-}\mathrm{V}$ rms source. The two loads draw a total real power of $400\,\mathrm{kW}$ at a power factor of $0.8$ lagging. One of the loads draws $120\,\mathrm{kW}$ at a power factor of $0.96$ leading. Find the complex power of the other load.

## Guide

Add and subtract complex powers as rectangular quantities. The total load is inductive, whereas the specified individual load is capacitive; the leading load's reactive power must be negative.

## Part a

Find the complex power of the other load.

### Solution

Lecture 2, page 13 defines complex power, and conservation of power gives

$$
S_T=S_1+S_2,\qquad Q=P\tan\theta,\qquad \cos\theta=\mathrm{pf}
$$

Use a positive angle for the lagging total and a negative angle for the leading $120\,\mathrm{kW}$ load. In $\mathrm{kW}$ and $\mathrm{kvar}$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_T&=400\tan(\cos^{-1}0.8)=400(0.75)=300\,\mathrm{kvar}\\
Q_1&=-120\tan(\cos^{-1}0.96)=-120\frac{0.28}{0.96}=-35\,\mathrm{kvar}\\
S_T&=400+j300\,\mathrm{kVA},\qquad S_1=120-j35\,\mathrm{kVA}
\end{aligned}
$$

Subtract the entire complex power of load 1:

$$
S_2=(400+j300)-(120-j35)=\boxed{280+j335\,\mathrm{kVA}}
$$
