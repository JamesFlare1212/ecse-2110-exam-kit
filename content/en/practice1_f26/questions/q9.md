## Question

A $1$-phase, $25\,\mathrm{kVA}$, $220/440\,\mathrm V$, $60\,\mathrm{Hz}$ transformer gave the following test results.

- Open circuit test ($440\,\mathrm V$ side open): $220\,\mathrm V$, $9{:}5\,\mathrm A$, $650\,\mathrm W$
- Short-circuit test ($220\,\mathrm V$ side shorted): $37{:}5\,\mathrm V$, $55\,\mathrm A$, $950\,\mathrm W$

## Guide

The open-circuit measurements are on the energized $220\,\mathrm V$ side, so they determine LV shunt parameters. The short-circuit measurements are on the energized $440\,\mathrm V$ side, so they determine HV series parameters. Use the square of the turns ratio to refer impedances; keep measured test current distinct from rated current.

## Part a

Determine the rated voltage and rated current for the high-voltage and low-voltage sides.

### Solution

The single-phase apparent-power rating defines rated current by

$$
S_N=V_NI_N,\qquad I_N=\frac{S_N}{V_N}
$$

Given $S_N=25000\,\mathrm{VA}$, the high-voltage winding is $440\,\mathrm V$ and the low-voltage winding is $220\,\mathrm V$. Substitute each rated voltage:

$$
\boxed{V_H=440\,\mathrm V,\quad I_H=\frac{25000}{440}=56.8182\,\mathrm A,\qquad
V_L=220\,\mathrm V,\quad I_L=\frac{25000}{220}=113.636\,\mathrm A}
$$

## Part b

Derive an approximate equivalent circuit referred to the HV as well as LV side.

### Solution

Use the test relations in [Lecture 6, page 8](/pages/lecture-06/8.webp), and the [ideal turns ratio](formula:turns-ratio). Neglect series drop in the open-circuit test and excitation current in the short-circuit test:

$$
R_c=\frac{V_{oc}^2}{P_{oc}},\quad I_c=\frac{P_{oc}}{V_{oc}},\quad
I_m=\sqrt{I_{oc}^2-I_c^2},\quad X_m=\frac{V_{oc}}{I_m},\quad
Z_{eq}=\frac{V_{sc}}{I_{sc}},\quad R_{eq}=\frac{P_{sc}}{I_{sc}^2},\quad
X_{eq}=\sqrt{Z_{eq}^2-R_{eq}^2}
$$

Interpret the printed “$9{:}5$” and “$37{:}5$” as $9.5\,\mathrm A$ and $37.5\,\mathrm V$. The open-circuit test energizes the LV winding, giving

$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{c,L}&=\frac{220^2}{650}=74.4615\,\Omega,\qquad I_c=\frac{650}{220}=2.95455\,\mathrm A\\
I_m&=\sqrt{9.5^2-2.95455^2}=9.02888\,\mathrm A\\
X_{m,L}&=\frac{220}{9.02888}=24.3663\,\Omega
\end{aligned}
$$

The short-circuit test energizes the HV winding. Using its measured $55\,\mathrm A$ rather than the rated $56.8182\,\mathrm A$ gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
Z_{eq,H}&=\frac{37.5}{55}=0.681818\,\Omega\\
R_{eq,H}&=\frac{950}{55^2}=0.314050\,\Omega\\
X_{eq,H}&=\sqrt{0.681818^2-0.314050^2}=0.605185\,\Omega
\end{aligned}
$$

For $a=V_H/V_L=440/220=2$, $Z_H=a^2Z_L=4Z_L$. Hence

$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{c,H}&=4(74.4615)=297.846\,\Omega,\quad X_{m,H}=4(24.3663)=97.4650\,\Omega\\
R_{eq,L}&=0.314050/4=0.0785124\,\Omega,\quad X_{eq,L}=0.605185/4=0.151296\,\Omega
\end{aligned}
$$

In each referred circuit put $R_c\parallel jX_m$ across the input, followed by the series branch $R_{eq}+jX_{eq}$ leading to the referred load. This gives

![HV- and LV-referred equivalent circuits](figure:p1-f26-q9-b-solution)

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\text{HV: }&R_c=297.846\,\Omega,\quad X_m=97.4650\,\Omega,\quad Z_{eq}=0.314050+j0.605185\,\Omega\\
\text{LV: }&R_c=74.4615\,\Omega,\quad X_m=24.3663\,\Omega,\quad Z_{eq}=0.0785124+j0.151296\,\Omega
\end{aligned}}
$$

### Source correction

The colon decimals are preserved in the question and interpreted explicitly here. The handwritten answer on PDF page 11 rounds the HV leakage reactance to about $0.603\,\Omega$; using the printed measurements without early rounding gives $0.605185\,\Omega$. The tests determine combined series parameters, not the separate resistance and leakage of each winding. The diagram in the original PDF belongs to the solution, so this article has no question figure. Page 12 calculates voltage regulation at a load condition not present in the printed prompt; it is not a distinct additional question and is not introduced as one.
