## Question

An inductive load consisting of $R$ and $X$ in series feeding from a $2400\text{-}\mathrm{V}$ rms supply absorbs $288\,\mathrm{kW}$ at a lagging power factor of $0.8$. Determine $R$ and $X$.

## Guide

The original diagram places resistance and inductive reactance in series, so both carry the supply current. Obtain the current from real power and power factor, then resolve the series impedance.

## Part a

Determine $R$ and $X$.

### Solution

[Lecture 2, page 13](/pages/lecture-02/13.webp) defines $S=VI^*=P+jQ$. For a series impedance this gives

$$
P=VI\cos\theta=I^2R,\qquad Q=VI\sin\theta=I^2X
$$

Given $V=2400\,\mathrm V$, $P=288000\,\mathrm W$ and lagging $\cos\theta=0.8$, the reactance is positive. Calculate the current and reactive power:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\theta&=\cos^{-1}(0.8)=36.8699^\circ\\
I&=\frac{288000}{2400(0.8)}=150\,\mathrm A\\
Q&=288000\tan36.8699^\circ=216000\,\mathrm{var}
\end{aligned}
$$

Substitute the same series current into both impedance components:

$$
\boxed{R=\frac{288000}{150^2}=12.8\,\Omega,\qquad X=\frac{216000}{150^2}=9.6\,\Omega}
$$
