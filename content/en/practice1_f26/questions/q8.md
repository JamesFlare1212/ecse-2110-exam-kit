## Question

A $1\varphi$, 5 kVA, 240/120 V, 60 Hz transformer has a core loss of 100 W at rated voltage and copper loss of 120 W at rated current. Calculate the efficiency for the following load condition:

## Guide

At rated voltage and frequency, take core loss as constant. Copper loss varies as the square of current, so the apparent-power load fraction determines the copper loss. Multiply kVA by power factor to obtain real output power.

## Part a

It is delivering 5 kVA at rated voltage and a power factor of 0.8.

### Solution

Use the reference sheet's [transformer efficiency](formula:transformer-efficiency). With load fraction $x=S_L/S_N$ and resistive winding losses,

$$
\eta=\frac{P_{\mathrm{out}}}{P_{\mathrm{out}}+P_c+P_{cu}},\qquad
P_{\mathrm{out}}=S_L\,\mathrm{pf},\qquad P_{cu}=x^2P_{cu,N}
$$

At 5 kVA, $x=5/5=1$, $P_c=100\,\mathrm W$ and $P_{cu,N}=120\,\mathrm W$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{\mathrm{out}}&=5000(0.8)=4000\,\mathrm W\\
P_{cu}&=1^2(120)=120\,\mathrm W\\
P_{\mathrm{in}}&=4000+100+120=4220\,\mathrm W
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore

$$
\eta=\frac{4000}{4220}\times100\%=\boxed{94.7867\%}
$$

## Part b

It is delivering 2 kVA at rated voltage and a power factor of 0.8.

### Solution

Use the same [efficiency relation](formula:transformer-efficiency), retaining full core loss and scaling copper loss with current squared:

$$
\eta=\frac{S_L\,\mathrm{pf}}{S_L\,\mathrm{pf}+P_c+x^2P_{cu,N}}
$$

At 2 kVA the load fraction is $x=2/5=0.4$. Thus

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{\mathrm{out}}&=2000(0.8)=1600\,\mathrm W\\
P_{cu}&=(0.4)^2(120)=19.2\,\mathrm W\\
P_{\mathrm{in}}&=1600+100+19.2=1719.2\,\mathrm W
\end{aligned}
$$

Substituting these powers gives

$$
\eta=\frac{1600}{1719.2}\times100\%=\boxed{93.0665\%}
$$
