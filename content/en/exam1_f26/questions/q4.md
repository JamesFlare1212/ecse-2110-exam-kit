## Question

Three loads are connected in parallel across a $12.47\,\mathrm{kV}$ three-phase supply.

- Load 1: Inductive load, $60\,\mathrm{kW}$ and $660\,\mathrm{kvar}$.
- Load 2: Capacitive load, $240\,\mathrm{kW}$ at $0.8$ power factor.
- Load 3: Resistive load of $60\,\mathrm{kW}$.

## Guide

Add the three complex powers using positive reactive power for the inductive load and negative reactive power for the capacitive load. Interpret the supply rating as line-to-line RMS voltage and use balanced three-phase power to obtain the line current. For compensation, keep real power fixed and find the reactive power appropriate to the target power factor before sizing the Y-connected bank.

## Part a

Find the total complex power, power factor, and the supply current.

### Solution

Use complex-power addition from [Lecture 2, PDF pages 13](/pages/lecture-02/13.webp)–[14](/pages/lecture-02/14.webp), and the formula sheet's [balanced three-phase power](formula:three-phase-power), also shown in [Lecture 3, PDF page 15](/pages/lecture-03/15.webp):

$$
S_T=\sum_k(P_k+jQ_k),\qquad Q=P\tan\theta,\qquad \mathrm{PF}=\frac{P}{|S|},\qquad S_T=3V_{an}I_a^*.
$$

The three stated powers are total three-phase quantities. Treat $12.47\,\mathrm{kV}$ as line-to-line RMS voltage, assume balanced loads as in the official solution, and choose $V_{an}$ as the zero-angle reference. The capacitive load has leading power factor, so its reactive power is negative:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\theta_2&=\cos^{-1}(0.8)=36.8699^\circ,\\
Q_2&=-240\tan36.8699^\circ=-180\,\mathrm{kvar},\\
S_1&=60+j660\,\mathrm{kVA},\quad S_2=240-j180\,\mathrm{kVA},\quad S_3=60+j0\,\mathrm{kVA},\\
S_T&=(60+240+60)+j(660-180+0)=360+j480\,\mathrm{kVA},\\
|S_T|&=\sqrt{360^2+480^2}=600\,\mathrm{kVA},\qquad \theta_T=\tan^{-1}\frac{480}{360}=53.1301^\circ.
\end{aligned}
$$

Compute phase voltage and conjugate the complex power when calculating current:

$$
\begin{aligned}
V_{an}&=\frac{12470}{\sqrt3}\angle0^\circ=7199.56\angle0^\circ\,\mathrm V,\\
I_a&=\frac{S_T^*}{3V_{an}^*}
=\frac{(360-j480)10^3}{\sqrt3(12470)}
=16.6677-j22.2236\,\mathrm A.
\end{aligned}
$$

The positive net reactive power makes the current lag the phase voltage:

$$
\boxed{S_T=(360+j480)\,\mathrm{kVA}=600\angle53.13^\circ\,\mathrm{kVA},\quad \mathrm{PF}=0.600\text{ lagging},\quad I_a=27.7795\angle-53.13^\circ\,\mathrm A.}
$$

### Source correction

The scanned question PDF contains a handwritten $48.115\,\mathrm A$ obtained by dividing apparent power by line voltage without the three-phase factor $\sqrt3$. The official solution instead uses $3V_{an}$ and obtains about $27.77\,\mathrm A$. The calculation above retains the specified $12.47\,\mathrm{kV}$ rather than rounding phase voltage to $7.2\,\mathrm{kV}$.

## Part b

A Y-connected capacitor bank is connected in parallel with the loads. Find the total $\mathrm{kvar}$ and the capacitance per phase in $\mu\mathrm F$ to improve the overall power factor to $0.8$ lagging. What is the new line current?

### Solution

Use the target power triangle and the capacitor relation $I_C=j\omega CV_{\mathrm{ph}}$. Substituting that current into $S=3V_{\mathrm{ph}}I_C^*$ gives the bank’s negative reactive power:

$$
Q'=P\tan(\cos^{-1}\mathrm{PF}'),\qquad Q_C=-3\omega C V_{\mathrm{ph}}^2=-\omega C V_L^2.
$$

From (a), $P=360\,\mathrm{kW}$ and $Q=480\,\mathrm{kvar}$. The target is $0.8$ lagging. The question does not specify frequency; adopt $f=60\,\mathrm{Hz}$ as the official solution does, while keeping the general frequency dependence explicit. First find the required reduction in reactive power:

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q'&=360\tan(\cos^{-1}0.8)=360(0.75)=270\,\mathrm{kvar},\\
Q_C&=Q'-Q=270-480=-210\,\mathrm{kvar},\\
C&=\frac{|Q_C|}{2\pi fV_L^2}
=\frac{210000}{2\pi(60)(12470)^2}\,\mathrm F
=3.5822449\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm F.
\end{aligned}
$$

Each Y capacitor sees $V_L/\sqrt3$, so the factor three is already included in the relation $|Q_C|=\omega CV_L^2$. Using $S'=360+j270\,\mathrm{kVA}$, calculate the new supply current:

$$
\begin{aligned}
|S'|&=\sqrt{360^2+270^2}=450\,\mathrm{kVA},\\
I_a'&=\frac{(360-j270)10^3}{\sqrt3(12470)}
=16.6677-j12.5008\,\mathrm A.
\end{aligned}
$$

The bank rating is quoted as a positive magnitude, while its absorbed reactive power is negative:

$$
\boxed{|Q_C|=210\,\mathrm{kvar}\ (Q_C=-210\,\mathrm{kvar}),\quad C=3.58224\left(\frac{60\,\mathrm{Hz}}{f}\right)\mu\mathrm F\text{ per phase},\quad I_a'=20.8346\angle-36.8699^\circ\,\mathrm A.}
$$

### Source correction

Frequency is absent from the printed prompt; $60\,\mathrm{Hz}$ is an explicit assumption from official solution PDF page 10. That solution gives $3.58\,\mu\mathrm F$ correctly, though its symbol $X_C$ is used for the complex impedance $-j740.48\,\Omega$. The handwritten answer in the question scan incorrectly uses $270\,\mathrm{kvar}$, the remaining load reactive power, in the capacitance calculation; the bank must provide $210\,\mathrm{kvar}$.
