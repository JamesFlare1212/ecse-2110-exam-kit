                    Short Answer Questions (30 points)

Question 1. (10 points) Why do transformers in operation produce a humming
noise?

Answer:

Transformers work on AC. The magnetic core experiences continuous magnetic reversals.
As a result, the core material is expanding and contracting repeatedly due to magnetic
striction producing vibrations and humming noise.

Question 3. (10 points) Describe the Aluminum Conductor with Steel
Reinforcement (ACSR) conductor and justify its structure.

Answer:

Aluminum Conductor with Steel Reinforcement (ACSR) conductor is a high-strength
stranded conductor primarily used for overhead power transmission and
distribution lines. Structurally, it consists of a solid or stranded galvanized steel core
at the center, surrounded by one or more outer layers of high-purity aluminum
strands.

The outer Aluminum strands provide high conducting path by using skin effect to
optimize the material. The inner galvanized steel core provides mechanical strength
to the conductor.
