                        Short Answer  Questions  (40  points)

Question    1.  (10  points) Why  the  field winding  coils  in   a synchronous  machine  are  usually

connected   in  series.





Answer:

Connecting  field winding  coils  in  series  ensures  that  the  same  magnitude  of current  flows
through   all  the  coils.  This  maintains  symmetry   in  the magnetic  field  created  by   all  the  poles.

Question   2.  (10  points) What    is synchronous  speed  and  why  synchronous

machines  can  operate   at synchronous  speed  only?


Answer:

When    a three-phase  winding   is supplied  by  3-phase  balanced  currents,  then   a rotating  magnetic
field   is established  which  rotates   at  a speed  equal  to  120    *   £ /P.  this  speed   is  called  the
synchronous  speed.  Here   f  is frequency  of 3-phase  currents  and  P   is  the number  of poles  of the
winding.

In   a synchronous  machine,  there   is  a magnetic  locking  between  stator  and  rotor magnetic  poles.
Since  stator  speed   is  fixed   at synchronous  speed,  the  rotor   is  also   at  the  same  speed.

Question   4.  (10  points)  Explain  about  the  loss  of synchronization    in synchronous
machines.





Answer:


In synchronous  machines,  the  stator  and  rotor  poles  are  locked  to  maintain  synchronization.  Due  any

reasons,  such  as overloading  etc.    if the  machine   lost  the  pole  lock  then  machine  goes  out  of

synchronization.
