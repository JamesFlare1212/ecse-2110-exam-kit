--- PAGE 16 ---
       Popular Semiconductor Materials

Silicon (Si):
Pros: Mature technology, low cost, and easily available
Cons: Relatively low switching frequencies, higher losses, and
relatively limited voltage and temperature capabilities
compared to SiC and GaN.

                      Gallium Nitride (GaN):
                       Pros:
                      Very high switching frequencies, high power density (smaller size for a given
                       power), and lower losses. Applicable for high-frequency applications like
                    consumer electronics and fast chargers.
                     Cons:
                          Relatively low voltage ratings and higher cost (than silicon)

Silicon Carbide (SiC):
Pros:
Very high breakdown voltage, high thermal conductivity, and high switching
frequencies. Applicable for high-power, high-temperature, and high-voltage
applications like electric vehicles and industrial power supplies.
Cons:
                                                                                    16More expensive than silicon or gallium nitride.

--- PAGE 17 ---
 Popular Semiconductor Materials



Summary:
 - Silicon is more suitable for a cost-effective solution for lower-
power, lower-voltage applications.

SiC is more suitable for high power, high voltage, and high
temperature applications.

GaN is more suitable for high switching frequencies, high
power density.




**SiC and GaN are considered "wide bandgap" materials. Better for higher voltage operation,
wider temperature ranges, and increased switching frequencies.
