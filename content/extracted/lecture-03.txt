--- PAGE 11 ---
Per-Phase Analysis Procedure

To do per-phase analysis
1.    Convert all -load/-sources to equivalent Y-’s


2.   Solve for phase “a” independent of the other phases

3.     If desired, phase “b” and “c” values can be determined by rotations (i.e.,
     ±120° degree phase shifts)


4.     If necessary, go back to original circuit to determine line-line values or
      internal -values.

Neutral shift – unbalanced 3-φ system

➢Neutral shift – shift between the neutral voltage of a Y-connected
  voltage source and a Y-connected load – may occur due to unbalanced
  currents when the two neutrals are not connected.


➢If the two neutrals are connected, the voltage between the neutral
  points is (nearly) zero and unbalanced (neutral) current flows through
  the connection.
