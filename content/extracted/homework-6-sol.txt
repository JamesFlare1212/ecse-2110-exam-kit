Question 1. (10 points) How salient-pole and round-rotor synchronous machines are different?


Answer: Salient pole machine has projected field poles as opposed to poles with smooth surface
on round rotor machines. The air-gap is not uniform in the case of a salient pole machine as
opposed to a smooth air-gap in the case of a round rotor machine. The direct and quadrature axis
reactances are the same in round rotor machine unlike the salient pole machine. There is a torque
due to saliency in salient pole machines but not in round rotor machines.

Question 2. (10 points) Explain the function of damper bars in a synchronous machine.


Answer:

While the load changes on a synchronous machine, the rotor will be subjected to speed
oscillations. The damper bars mainly help damp the oscillations in the rotor of a synchronous
machine.
