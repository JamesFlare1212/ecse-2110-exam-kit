Question 1. (40 points) Write a short paragraph to answer.

    a.  (8 points) What are the advantages of electrical energy over other forms of energy?
      Answer:

      The advantages of electrical energy over other forms of energy are as follows:
           - Long distance and very fast transmission
           - Easy to control
           - High efficiency and reliability
           - Very clean system
           - Used in numerous applications

    b.  (8 points) Why AC is preferred over DC power systems?

      Answer:


      AC is preferred over DC for the following reasons:
           - It is easy to step up or step down the generated voltage using transformers. Higher voltages
       help reduce transmission stage electrical losses.
           - 3-phase AC can transmit more power for the given amount of material.
           - AC generation, repair, and maintenance are generally cheaper than DC systems.
           - AC motors, particularly induction motors, are robust and cheaper.

    c.  (8 points) Why is the tolerance on power system frequency relatively very small?

      Answer:

      The tolerance on power system frequency is relatively small for the following reasons:
           - Keep AC machines in AC synchrony
           - Machines are designed to be their most efficient at synchronous frequency, large deviations
        increase wear and can cause damage
           - Power output is directly proportional to this frequency

    d.  (8 points) Why is electrical power transmission carried out at higher voltages?

      Answer:

        Electric power is a function of product of voltage and current. For a given amount of
       power, higher the voltage, lower will be the current. Low currents cause less transmission
       stage losses. Thus, to minimize transmission stage losses, higher voltage is preferred at
       transmission stage.
