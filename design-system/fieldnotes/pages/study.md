# Study workspace

These study-workspace rules refine and override the [Fieldnotes master rules](../MASTER.md). They describe the engineering reading interface, not a generated portfolio template.

- Product name: Fieldnotes. Course code, bilingual title and resource navigation come from `content/course.json`; the current course is ECSE 2110 / 电能系统. Keep an academic three-column reading workspace, not a portfolio page pattern.
- Warm paper background, white surfaces, dark ink text, restrained burnt orange primary action, teal formula references. Define all colors as semantic tokens in the existing global CSS.
- Latin headings: Georgia or a locally available serif. Interface and bilingual body: self-hosted Inter plus system Chinese sans. Reading text uses the shared 16 px reader / 14 px supporting / 12 px metadata scale; native Dialog titles share an 18 px heading treatment. KaTeX owns mathematical typography. Embed its two small critical WOFF2 faces (Main Regular and Math Italic) in the stylesheet so inline formulas do not reflow through partially loaded font combinations. Other faces remain separate assets; count both embedded and fetched font bytes in the 200 KiB reading budget. No external font requests. Keep closing prose punctuation with its inline formula rather than leaving a punctuation-only line.
- Desktop: approximately 250 px navigation, readable central document, 300 px source rail. At 1180 px and below move the rail into a Sheet; at 800 px and below use a navigation Sheet. Keep a full document flow and visible focus.
- Both desktop sidebars can collapse independently from persistent top-bar panel buttons, following the shadcn block trigger pattern. Keep the reader centered at a comfortable maximum width, preserve panel content state and save preferences locally. Collapsed panels are inert; on compact screens these controls open the existing Sheets. Ctrl/Cmd+B toggles exam navigation. Question links within the same Exam tab preserve the navigation list's independent scroll position across Astro swaps and history traversal, including after manually opening another paper. Archive accordions open immediately, without replayable height animations; solution accordions keep their native motion. Switching Exam tabs replaces the directory and starts at the top. Question-link pressed states must not translate rows. Keep URL updates and reading-focus behavior intact.
- The top-bar breadcrumb follows course → Exam group → paper → current problem. The course link returns to the configured course entry; the Exam and paper menus expose their papers and questions with real problem URLs and Astro navigation. Only the last item is the current page. Keep the top bar on one row, adapting to the workspace's actual width rather than the viewport. At constrained widths omit the search caption/shortcut and use a labeled course icon; on narrow screens collapse ancestors into a keyboard-accessible path menu that retains the course entry, papers and questions. Keep full labels accessible when truncated, and transfer focus to a visible trigger if resizing hides an open menu's trigger. Do not repeat the course title / “study desk” eyebrow above the problem.
- Native shadcn-svelte primitives: breadcrumbs, dropdown menus, tabs, toggle groups, accordion, tooltip, dialog, sheet, popover, field, progress, buttons, inputs. Preserve their native semantics, keyboard behavior, tokens and variants.
- Illustrations: standard circuit symbols use pinned Schemdraw 0.23 on Matplotlib coordinates; waveforms, power-angle curves and phasor constructions use Matplotlib. Deliver reproducible static SVG with native text, plus an original figure switch. Technical labels use standard symbols in both languages; omit boilerplate recreation captions. Integrate the source-exam action as a compact, labeled page button beside “Original” in the shared figure-version track; do not reserve a separate footer row. Keep that page action outside the radio group (never nest interactive controls), retain its localized tooltip and return focus after closing the source viewer. Preserve drawing/card height when switching versions. The figure-version selector shares the Exam tabs' segmented track and active-item styles while keeping exclusive radio selection and horizontal arrow-key navigation.
- Solution figures render at their explicit standalone `figure:` Markdown image paragraphs inside the authored derivation flow. Use the existing Figure component and its enlargement in place, with an 8 px figure margin inside the solution body's shared rhythm, rather than a second trailing copy. Legacy articles without placement markers keep their prior fallback only for compatibility. Worked-solution placement guidance belongs to `docs/solution-style.md`.
- Figure cards are centered and adapt to each SVG's intrinsic dimensions, label density and available reading width, rather than sharing fixed width/height caps. Show the complete drawing at its original aspect ratio in the article, with natural height and no internal scrolling, forced minimum image size or decorative grid. Keep the drawing height stable when switching to the original crop. The enlarged dialog uses each drawing's own readable size (at least 10 px for its smallest SVG label), confines detail scrolling to the image region, and offers a native Fit width toggle for a complete overview on narrow screens. Toolbars and source actions must fit or wrap within the card in both languages.
- Before derivations, introduce the concepts, actual givens, goals and starting approach in the selected language, with links to the relevant lecture PDF pages. Keep the introduction separate from the original exam wording.
- Authored prompt quantities, symbols and units use explicit KaTeX just like calculations. Parallel loads, transformer tests, characteristics and schedules are real Markdown lists, not paragraphs separated by blank breaks. RichText keeps single-paragraph fragments inline and uses an 8 px flow for multiple blocks; display math clears its default margins and confines any overflow to its own formula. Preserve literal source anomalies such as colon decimals.
- Formulas: comfortable display spacing, horizontal scrolling confined to a formula, units upright. Keep the terms of one sum or difference together when they fit at normal reading size; reserve aligned continuation rows for meaningful calculation stages and simplification. Place KaTeX boxed results inside the final derivation step, without an answer card or extra border. Avoid shrinking entire pages to fit mobile.
- Worked solutions use a consistent 8 px gap between paragraphs and equation groups, with small equation insets and no stacked paragraph or display-math margins. Reserve scroll-hint space only for formulas that actually overflow; preserve math font sizes and the authored rows within each derivation.
- Authored solution style, complete integration working and bilingual examples are defined only in [docs/solution-style.md](../../../docs/solution-style.md). Render that flow directly; do not invent numbered steps or a second answer model in the UI.
- Formula names in lead-in prose are inline source links, inheriting the paragraph’s font and line height with a subtle underline. Use the native Hover Card for preview and preserve source-page image navigation. Do not repeat icon labels beside equations.
- Use one solution view without study/practice modes. Each part opens its full solution at once, including all calculations, the final answer, solution figure and source note. Parts start collapsed on ordinary problem navigation; a link or search result targeting a part opens and focuses that part. The global toggle collapses all if any part is open, otherwise reveals all. Preserve retired mode preferences and legacy saved step counts without applying them to the UI. Completion, bookmarks, locale and theme persist locally; source reading uses same-origin page images; original PDF download/open actions are retired and PDF files are excluded from publication. Practice is done on paper; omit note-taking and per-part text entry.
- Titles and question wording follow the global locale. Chinese is a faithful translation of the original exam; English retains the original wording. Use a single question block with no separate translation switch or supplemental panel.
- Author papers separately in `content/en/` and `content/zh-cn/`, with single-language Markdown and complete independent paper assets in each. Global language switching selects that language's SVGs, original crops and source-page images while retaining the same problem URL, theme and progress.
- Guides and solutions are natural Markdown. Pair translated questions and parts by stable ID; allow each language its own paragraphs and formula groups. Render the authored flow directly, with one final boxed calculation and no numbered step wrappers or duplicate answer model.
- Source links show a cropped original excerpt on hover/focus and open a page viewer with a percentage-based bounding rectangle. Page numbers always mean 1-based PDF pages. Hover previews and reference-rail metadata use compact, localized configured sheet names (e.g. `Exam 2 公式表` / `Exam 2 sheet`) plus an indivisible `p.1` label, without uppercasing a full provenance title or repeating the term. Keep full titles unchanged in document metadata and the keyboard/touch-accessible source viewer; genuinely long fallback titles may wrap without forcing page-wide overflow.
- Prompt diagrams exist only when printed in the actual question. Set `figure: null` for text-only questions; never add an illustrative diagram there. All pedagogical schematics and requested plots belong only to revealed solutions and identify any illustrative parameters in their captions. Recreated source geometries preserve the source's symbolic dimensions.
- Dialog return paths preserve focus: closing a library opened from a compact Sheet returns to the matching visible toolbar control, never to an exiting Sheet or the document body. Desktop library and nested source dialogs return to their original live trigger; selecting a problem keeps destination focus.
- Source viewer: use native 36 px pagination buttons, page input and zoom buttons. The percentage is a native reset-to-page-fit button; zoom range derives from the available reader width and delivered image detail so narrow screens are not capped at an unreadable 200%. Keep local keyboard/touch scrolling and align an oversized citation to its beginning, not an empty middle. At sufficient width and height, show a vertical, independently scrolling list of all page thumbnails. Highlight and reveal the selected page when navigation changes it; clicks change pages, while the current thumbnail also supports pointer/keyboard positioning within the main page. Keep the original citation and current viewport markers. Hide this overview on compact screens.
- Ghost actions use the semantic `--interactive-hover*` tokens for an opaque, visibly distinct hover/focus surface, a defined hover border and readable muted labels in both themes. Do not dilute dark hover fills with opacity. Keep the native focus ring, preserve control dimensions and use the shared Button variant rather than per-row color overrides.
- Motion is subtle and respects reduced motion; no visual reliance on color alone.

- Keep canonical question and solution diagrams as static SVG. The specifically approved Q4(a) semi-converter may add a native Svelte progressive interaction at its existing solution-figure marker: static Schemdraw sprite fragments, synchronized normalized waveforms, explicit step/play/pause/scrub controls, global locale/theme, and no autoplay. Retain the complete static Figure for no-JS and loading failure. In the enhanced view, Full plot opens the shared Figure dialog directly, initially fitting the entire sheet to the window with a readable-detail toggle; never append another full image card to the article. Keep the four conduction ranges on one compact row, group transport and a freely scrubbable linear 0°–360° progress slider (0.1° input precision, no interval detents). Keep all five reference marks at their true angular positions: 0°, 30°, 180°, 210°, 360°; never remove the 30°/210° firing marks. Stagger labels on narrow tracks instead of hiding them or turning them into snap stops. Play runs from the current position through every intervening interval to 360°, never pausing at firing or zero-crossing boundaries. Keep pause/resume, explicit restart and boundary-jump arrows; hold the final interval at the completed endpoint rather than wrapping its selection. Reduced motion skips animation and exposes the cycle end or manual scrubbing. Keep circuit strokes legible at 1.8 screen pixels via non-scaling stroke. Retain the compact controls, and disclose extended assumptions/explanations through the native reading-help Popover rather than stacking duplicate prose. Cancel playback on collapse, offscreen reading, dialogs, navigation, locale/theme changes and reduced motion; never store animation state in progress records. This is not a blanket migration of other figures to animation. Construct perspective solids in 3D and project their faces, dimensions and coordinate axes through one orthographic camera. Preserve the original figure switch and course-specific solution drawings; omit interactive 3D viewers and their controls.
- Scientific diagram labels are large and bold, including math symbols; position them clear of arrows. Navigate questions through the sidebar or the breadcrumb directory menus; omit a bottom previous/next pager.
